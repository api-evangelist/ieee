This white paper gives distribution engineers and utility teams a practical overview of the substation exit, the short and high-consequence section where station capacity divides into individual feeders. It explains how covered, spacer-supported overhead construction can reduce common contact-driven faults while leaving room for future circuits. What you will learn about: Why a fault near the substation exposes more customers than one farther along the feeder, and why the first spans out of the station carry so much reliability weight.
