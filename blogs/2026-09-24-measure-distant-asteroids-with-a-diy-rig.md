I’ve seen two total solar eclipses and have been duly impressed by what happens as the moon casts its shadow on Earth. But recently I’ve become even more intrigued by a similar phenomenon that doesn’t involve the sun or the moon—something called an asteroid occultation. That’s what happens when an asteroid orbits around the solar system and blocks the light of a distant star you’re viewing from Earth.
